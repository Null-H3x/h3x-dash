⚠ DEPLOYMENT LIABILITY STATEMENT

Use of this module is authorized only when at least one of the three scenarios below applies to the operator's intended use. The scenarios are alternatives — not joint requirements — but each carries its own conditions.

SCENARIO A
LAB / OWNED INFRASTRUCTURE
  • Operating inside a controlled, isolated training range or on systems you personally own (your name on the hardware / lease / service agreement).
  • The lab is segmented from production and public networks; no inadvertent traffic crosses the boundary.
  • RF-capable devices (O.MG, Pineapple, LAN Turtle 3G) stay inside the authorized RF perimeter.
SCENARIO B
CONTRACTED AUTHORIZED PENTEST
  • You hold a current, signed Rules of Engagement / penetration-testing agreement with the authorized system owner (verbal or implied permission is insufficient).
  • Target IPs / SSIDs / scope, the deployment window, and acceptable outcomes are documented in that agreement.
  • You are physically present for any device deployment and the device is recovered before the window closes.
SCENARIO C
SECURITY RESEARCH
  • Operating against a research lab, vulnerable-by-design platform (Metasploitable, HackTheBox, VulnHub, TryHackMe, etc.), or your own published-research target.
  • Findings will be disclosed responsibly per the platform's or vendor's policy.
  • No third-party data leaves the research environment.
